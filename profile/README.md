# Things They Don't Teach You in School

Free, AI-tutored courses on the practical stuff school skipped: tax, money, politics, health,
nutrition. Your AI assistant asks what you need, downloads only the courses for your country, and
teaches you at your own pace.

> **Under development.** Courses have not yet been reviewed by professionals. Don't rely on them yet.

- **Learners:** start with [enrol](https://github.com/things-they-dont-teach-you/enrol).
- **Contributors:** build or fix courses with the [staff-room](https://github.com/things-they-dont-teach-you/staff-room).
- **Professionals:** help us review lessons in your field. [Get in touch](https://things-they-dont-teach-you.github.io/#contribute).

Website: [things-they-dont-teach-you.github.io](https://things-they-dont-teach-you.github.io)
